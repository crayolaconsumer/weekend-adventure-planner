## Google App campaign (the $400 credit)

A "Google App campaign" (formerly Universal App Campaign) doesn't use manually-designed banners — you give Google **text + images + video** and it auto-assembles ads across Search, Play, YouTube, Display & Discover. So your job is just to upload the assets below.

### Headlines — up to 5, max 30 chars each
1. `Stop scrolling. Start roaming` (29)
2. `Beat boredom in one tap` (23)
3. `Real places near you` (20)
4. `Plan your day in seconds` (24)
5. `Things to do near you` (21)

### Descriptions — up to 5, max 90 chars each
1. `Tap "I'm Bored" and discover real places near you — food, parks, culture, nights out.` (85)
2. `Swipe to save spots you love, then plan a spontaneous day out in seconds. Free to use.` (85)
3. `See what's on near you — gigs, markets and family days out — plus where friends love.` (85)
4. `Less doom-scrolling, more real adventures. Discover your city with ROAM, free.` (77)
5. `Build a map of places you love and never run out of things to do this weekend.` (78)

### Image assets (in `marketing-assets/ads/`)
| File | Ratio | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `ad-landscape-1200x628.png` | 1.91:1 | required landscape |
| `ad-square-1200x1200.png` | 1:1 | required square |
| `ad-portrait-1080x1350.png` | 4:5 | portrait placements |

You can also upload the 6 store screenshots from `framed/play/` as extra image assets — Google rotates whatever performs best.

### Video asset
`video/ROAM-promo-1080x1920.mp4` — upload to **YouTube** (unlisted is fine), then add the YouTube URL to the campaign. Same URL also goes in **Play Console → Store listing → Video** to unlock your feature graphic.

### Suggested setup (App install campaign)
1. ads.google.com → New campaign → **App promotion** → **App installs** → choose ROAM (iOS and/or Android).
2. Add the headlines, descriptions, the 3 images, the 6 Play screenshots, and the YouTube video.
3. Bidding: **Target cost per install (tCPI)**. Start ~£1.50–£3.00 and let it learn.
4. Budget: with a £/$400 credit, pace **~£10–£13/day for ~30 days** (App campaigns need volume to exit the learning phase — don't set it too low).
5. Geo: United Kingdom. Language: English.
6. Leave it ~2 weeks before judging — App campaigns optimise slowly.

> Note: the AdSense flag on go-roam.uk is a **separate** product (you getting paid to show ads) and does **not** affect this campaign (you paying to advertise).

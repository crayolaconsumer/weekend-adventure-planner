# ROAM - launch kit (copy ready to paste)

Screenshots in `assets/` (captured from the live site).

## 1. Product Hunt
Tagline: ROAM - stop scrolling, start roaming. The free app for finding local places and events worth getting out for.
First comment (founder):
"I built ROAM because I wanted an answer to 'what should we do this weekend?' that isn't an Instagram feed. It's built on real map data and local venues, it's free, and local organisers can put their event in front of the people nearby. It's early - the first cities are seeded, and I'm adding more towns as the community grows. Feedback welcome, and tell me your city if you want it covered."
Body:
"ROAM is a free weekend planner for the UK. Swipe through curated local places - parks, cafés, attractions, trails - and a What's On feed of local events, then build a weekend plan around them. Built on OpenStreetMap data. Local venues and organisers can promote their event to people nearby (flat pricing, no commission). Free to use; ROAM+ removes ads and unlocks bigger search radii. iOS, Android and web."

## 2. Show HN (one paragraph, no hype)
"Show HN: ROAM - a free weekend planner built on OpenStreetMap. It shows you local places and a What's On feed of events near you, and local venues can promote their event to people nearby. I'm one person building it; I'd like feedback on what's useful from anyone who lives in a UK city it already covers. Site: go-roam.uk"

## 5. OpenStreetMap community PR (post to OSM blog or local users group newsletter)
"Building ROAM on OpenStreetMap: I'm building ROAM, a free weekend planner for the UK. It's built directly on OSM data - the places people save and rate come from OpenStreetMap places, and I want to give that data a job: get people out to the places it describes. ROAM is one person's project, free, and I'm happy to answer how the data is used. If your city's OSM mapping is thin, tell me - I'll prioritise seeding it."
